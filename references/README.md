# References

Drop your images here. PNG or JPG. Screenshots of interfaces, designs, layouts, anything you admire.

**Good references:**
- Screenshots of interfaces you love using
- Design work that makes you feel something specific
- Layouts that feel "right" even if you can't explain why
- Pages where every decision feels intentional

**How many:**
- Minimum: 3 (enough to find a pattern)
- Sweet spot: 5-7 (clear signal without noise)
- Maximum: 10 (more than this dilutes the signal)

**Tips:**
- Quality over quantity. 5 references with a clear thread beats 15 random screenshots.
- Include full-page screenshots, not cropped details. The agent needs to see layout, spacing, and proportion.
- Mix is fine but inconsistency lowers the confidence score. If you put a Muji page next to a brutalist portfolio, the agent will try to reconcile them. That's interesting but it will be confused.
- Your references ARE your input. There's no spec, no brief, no instructions. Just these images.
